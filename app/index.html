THE ROUTE TO LEGEND

Platinum Roadmap

Budget about 200 hours at roughly 4/10 difficulty. Let the story lead, then give the whole state your attention.

  1. FIRST / STORY

    Keep your options open

    Play the story first. Make a separate manual save at the start of every chapter, because there is no mission replay. Track every meditation spot for Positive Mental Attitude before advancing.

  2. NEXT / MOP-UP

    Settle the loose ends

    Finish stranger strands, partner activities, races, and combat tasks. Filter by unearned trophies to pick a manageable goal for each session.

  3. LAST / THE 100% PUSH

    Make Leonida yours

    Use the in-game completion record to close remaining categories. Sweep collectibles and stunts by area, buy the remaining garages, and finish the long counters on your way to 100%.

A note on secrets

Some hidden methods remain unconfirmed. Their guides say so explicitly; a clue is not a guaranteed unlock route.
